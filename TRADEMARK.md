# Kylrix Trademark & Brand Policy

## Strict Branding Policy
When building tools, integrations, mobile apps, or desktop wrappers on Kylrix infrastructure:
- **Never use the name "Kylrix" or any confusingly similar branding** in your application name, domain, npm package, or public app store listing.
- Acceptable naming examples: *"SuperNotes for Kylrix"*, *"VaultSync (powered by Kylrix)"*, *"MyClient for Kylrix API"*.
- Unacceptable naming examples: *"Kylrix Mobile"*, *"Kylrix Desktop"*, *"Kylrix Client"*, *"KylrixApp"*.

Failure to adhere will result in immediate revocation of OAuth2 client IDs and suspension of associated developer accounts.

## Notice to Users
The **only** official clients provided and maintained by the Kylrix team are:
- The web app: [kylrix.space](https://www.kylrix.space)
- The official CLI: [`@kylrix/cli`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@kylrix/cli)

Consciously using unofficial third-party clients that mimic the Kylrix brand risks credential theft, data tampering, and account suspension.
